Which words drive the answer?

Each word is removed in turn. The percentage is how much the model's answer changes without it. Normal prompts spread their influence across several words. A backdoor trigger takes nearly all of it.

How the answer forms, layer by layer

After each layer we ask the model, "if you had to answer now, what would you say?" Early layers make rough guesses. Watch for the layer where the final answer appears.

Where each layer is looking

Whole-model scan

The scan doesn't need a prompt or a guess. It slips every one of the model's 50,257 words into eight unrelated sentences. A normal word leads them to different endings. A trigger forces the same ending every time. Words scoring above 50% (dashed line) are flagged.
